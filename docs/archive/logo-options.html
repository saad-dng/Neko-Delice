Comparison & Recommendation

The brand name is spelled strictly NEKO DELICE. In all three lockups, the wordmark is rendered in live text using self-hosted Cormorant Garamond 600 with wide tracking (0.22em for NEKO, 0.28em for DELICE), rendered in rich espresso (#2B211A) on warm cream (#FBF6EE), yielding a contrast ratio exceeding 13:1 (WCAG AAA).
